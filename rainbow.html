What Makes a Rainbow

  • Rainbows are formed when light from the sun is scattered by water droplets (e.g. raindrops or fog) through a
  • process called refraction. Refraction occurs when the light from the sun changes direction when passing through
  • medium denser than air, such as a raindrop.
